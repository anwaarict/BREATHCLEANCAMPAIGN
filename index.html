Why It Matters

Air pollution is a silent crisis

Every day, millions of people breathe air that exceeds safe pollution limits. Vehicle emissions, factory smoke, crop burning, and unchecked waste all contribute to a growing public health emergency.

In cities like Lahore, air quality regularly ranks among the worst in the world — causing respiratory disease, heart problems, and reducing life expectancy for entire communities.

🫁 The World Health Organization estimates that air pollution causes 7 million premature deaths every year globally. Yet it remains largely invisible to daily life.

What We Did

Our activities on the ground

As part of our Civics project, our team took real, tangible action — not just words on paper.

🌱

Tree Plantation Drive

We organized a tree plantation drive at our school and local community park. Trees are nature's air filters — absorbing CO₂ and releasing clean oxygen.

50+ trees planted
📋

Pamphlet Distribution

Designed and printed educational pamphlets explaining the causes and health effects of air pollution, and distributed them door-to-door in our neighborhood.

150+ distributed
📢

Awareness Campaign

We ran a public awareness campaign with banners, street walks, and speeches to educate people about the dangers of smog and how to reduce it daily.

Community-wide
🎯

School Presentations

Delivered awareness sessions in classrooms, sharing data on Lahore's air quality and encouraging students to take small daily actions for cleaner air.

Multiple classes
🎯

Rickshaw Drivers Survey

Met multiple local rickshaw drivers and informed them regarding air pollution and encouraged them to contribute to the environment.

Multiple classes
📋

Posters Installation

We installed multiple informative and awareness posters on walls and on trees throughout our university and local neighbourhoods

200+ distributed
📋

Online Survey

We conducted an online survey which asked questions regarding air pollution to collect data for our initiative

200+ distributed
📢

Roadside SMD Advertisements

Our team ran ads throughout the populous and crowded parts of the city to raise awareness about air and environmental protection

200+ distributed
Our Impact

What we hope to change

🌳

Greener Neighbourhoods

Every tree we plant absorbs up to 22 kg of CO₂ per year, improving local air quality over the long term.

🧠

Informed Citizens

When people understand the causes of pollution, they make better daily choices — carpooling, less burning, more planting.

🏃

Healthier Futures

Reducing pollution means fewer respiratory illnesses, especially for children and the elderly who are most vulnerable.

🤝

Community Action

Civic awareness creates ripple effects. One informed family influences neighbors, workplaces, and local leaders.
